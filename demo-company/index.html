How we work

A simple four-step process.

  1. 1

    Discovery call

    A 30-minute chat about your goals, customers and budget. You get a fixed quote within two working days.

  2. 2

    Design

    We share page designs for desktop and mobile, then refine together until you're happy.

About the studio

A small studio that treats your website like it's our own.

Meridian Studio is a three-person design and development team based in Singapore. We started because too many small businesses were paying for slow, template-heavy websites that didn't bring in customers.

We keep projects small and personal: you work directly with the people designing and building your site, timelines are honest, and every site we ship is fast, accessible and easy for you to update.
